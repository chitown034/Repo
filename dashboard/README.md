# Venture Command Center

Single-file dashboard (`index.html`) for running a veteran-owned HoldCo and its ventures:
Military Family Office, AI company, real estate, mortgage broker/MLO, foundation, nonprofit,
research grants, gov contracts & grants, buy-a-business, and the acquisition HoldCo.

Tabs: Incentives & Tax (state incentives with TX/NC/GA/OH/LA examples, how to find and apply,
SBA loan types and 2025-26 SOP changes, QSBS, Section 179 and bonus depreciation, historic
rehabilitation credit, New Markets Tax Credit, Opportunity Zones, R&D credit, government-backed
acquisition financing, debt leverage calculator, incentives tracker), Overview (with a Needs Attention alerts panel), Scorecard (13-week weekly measures with
goal coloring and trend lines), CEO Desk (time allocation, decision log, key relationships),
Legal & Risk (risk register, insurance matrix, legal docs, data security), Wealth & Exit (net worth,
venture valuations, exit options, veteran benefits, tax and estate checklists), Marketing (brand,
channels, ad rules, funnel and content calendar), Ventures (KPIs + 4-phase execution checklists per venture), Funding, Acquisitions
(deal board, SBA 7(a) + seller-note calculator, buy box, due diligence), Grants, Certifications (SDVOSB/VetCert and 8(a) step-by-step
guides, document checklists, certification menu and tracker, veteran loan and grant preferences,
programs-from-videos tracker), Veteran GovCon
(VetCert, VR&E, Boots to Business, VBOC, bid tracker), SME Operator (Integrator scorecard, hiring,
30-60-90), Automation, New Ventures (idea scoring), Roadmap (36-month plan, quarterly rocks, venture synergies),
Finance (consolidated P&L, entity map, compliance calendar, deadlines), Calculators (VA house hack,
GovCon labor rate, MLO income, foundation payout, public support test), Playbooks (scaling stages,
meeting agenda, decision rights, SOP library), Resources (65+ official links), Backup.

Each venture page also has: ideal customers, revenue streams, key numbers, first-90-day checklist,
risks and mitigations, SOPs to write, team build with hire triggers, and a venture-specific tracker
(loan pipeline, property portfolio, client households, customer pipeline, donors, grants made).

Workflow automation: Action Center (unified task list with recurring tasks, 13 one-click workflows,
15 automation rules that create follow-ups from tracker changes, automation log, weekly brief),
quick capture with smart prefixes (Ctrl/Cmd+K search, C to capture), AI & Templates (portfolio Q&A
that can add tasks, AI deal screener, AI capture routing, 11 outreach templates with AI
personalization), CSV import/export and JSON backup.

When opened as a claude.ai artifact the page can also: sync data across devices (private per-user
store), use Claude for AI features, take voice capture, save files, and create Google Calendar events
and Gmail drafts. Each asks permission on first use. Opened as a plain file, data stays in this
browser's localStorage; use the Backup tab to copy/restore it.
Open `index.html` in any browser; no build step.
