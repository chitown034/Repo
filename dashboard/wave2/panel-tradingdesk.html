      Market viewer — OpenStock (runs on your own computer)

      An open-source stock viewer: watchlists, live-style charts, heatmap, company profiles and news, with your own login and your own local database. It is a viewer, not a broker — it cannot place a trade, and the paper desk above stays separate (it reads your own price files). Runs on that computer only (127.0.0.1); nothing is exposed to the internet.

      Open OpenStock
      1. One time, in Terminal on the computer: paste the install command (needs Docker Desktop and Node; it prints what is missing).
      2. Get a free Finnhub key at finnhub.io/register and type it into ~/Applications/OpenStock/.env after NEXT_PUBLIC_FINNHUB_API_KEY= — never into a chat.
      3. Paste the start command, then click Open OpenStock and create your local account.
