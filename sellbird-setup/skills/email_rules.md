# Email rules for Steven Shearrill (mortgage broker) — read before writing any email

These replace Sellbird's built-in email shape. Sellbird's send gate still enforces 220 words, one link and its banned phrases.

## Who we email
- Business partners only: real estate brokers and agents, home builders, property managers, CPAs, financial advisors, estate-planning attorneys.
- Never a consumer, homeowner or borrower. If the prospect looks like an individual consumer, don't write; flag it for Steven.
- Steven is licensed in CA, IL, NV, FL and AZ. Only write to businesses in those states (the target list is Southern California).

## Mortgage advertising rules (federal and California)
- Never state a rate, APR, payment, points, fees, down payment amount or loan terms. Those are Reg Z / MAP Rule trigger terms and don't belong in cold email.
- Never say or imply "guaranteed approval", "lowest rate", "no credit check", "pre-approved" (for the recipient's clients), or any government affiliation ("VA-approved lender", "government program for you").
- Every email ends with this sign-off, exactly:
  Steven Shearrill
  Mortgage Broker · NMLS 2449246 · CA DRE #01988316
  Patriot Pacific Financial Corp · NMLS 1921615
- No fair-lending risk: never mention or target race, color, religion, national origin, sex, familial status, disability, age, marital status or any other protected class.

## RESPA Section 8 — referral partners
- Never offer, imply or hint at anything of value for referrals: no referral fees, gifts, meals, paid leads, paid co-marketing, "taking care of" anyone, or splitting costs.
- If asked what Steven pays for referrals, the answer is that federal law doesn't allow it. The value is service: fast pre-approvals, on-time closings, updates to the agent.

## Shape
- Email 1: under 75 words. One specific observation about their business from their own website or listings (a recent sale, a new community, a VA-heavy area, a hiring post), then one question. No pitch paragraph.
- Email 2 (a few days later): a different, useful angle, such as how a broker helps when a buyer is self-employed, a veteran or needs non-QM. Under 70 words.
- Email 3: a short, polite close-out. Under 40 words.
- At most one link, and only patriotpacific.com, patriotpacificmlo.com or valoaneducator.tv.
- Subject lines: short, lowercase-friendly, specific to them; no clickbait, no "Re:" or "Fwd:" tricks.
- Voice: direct, warm, plain English; short sentences; no exclamation marks; no buzzwords.
