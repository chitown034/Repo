How this works

Sound is generated in your browser, so there is nothing to install. Click an instrument to hear it, or use your computer keyboard on the piano.

Scope, plainly: a dashboard cannot hold every song's full tabs or sheet music. Those are copyrighted and run to thousands of pages. Instead, each artist guide teaches the sound: instruments, chords, rhythm and technique, plus a graded song list so you know what to learn first. For exact note-for-note charts, use licensed sources linked in the Artist Guides tab.

Drum machine and turntable

Beat Lab

Click a step to turn it on. Click a row name to hear that sound. Presets are inspired by each style's feel, not copies of any record.

Scratch deck

Press and drag the platter back and forth. Faster movement gives a higher pitch, like a record scratch.

What each sound does

Kick sets the low pulse. Snare and clap mark the backbeat on 2 and 4 (steps 5 and 13 in the grid). Hats keep time and add speed. 808 is a long, tuned bass drum used as a bass note.

Swing pushes every second 16th note late, which gives boom bap its lazy bounce.

Try this: load Boom Bap, then add one extra kick on step 11 and listen to how the feel changes.

Camelot wheel: harmonic mixing

Click a key to see which tracks mix well with it.

Top row (B) is major keys, bottom row (A) is minor. Tracks that share a number, or sit one step away on the same letter, blend without clashing.
