# About Steven — always-true background

Short on purpose. The full record is the Second Brain (see `CLAUDE.md`); the rows this file rests on are linked at the end.

## Who
- Steven runs a mortgage business (Patriot Pacific Financial) and a real estate business (LPT Realty) in the San Diego / Temecula / Inland Empire corridor, alongside MBSE (model-based systems engineering) work, nonprofit involvement, NQ/ES futures trading, and family-office and wealth planning.
- He works with Isa (assistant) and a TC (transaction coordinator). The AI Team reaches him only through Vanessa.

## How he works — the four-layer leverage model
| Layer | Who | What belongs there |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Steven | Licensed, relationship-heavy, judgment-intensive work: negotiations, client relationships, strategy, final authority |
| 2 | Isa / assistants | Structured human operations, exceptions, QC |
| 3 | AI | Orchestration, drafting, extraction, monitoring |
| 4 | Systems of record | Definitive transactional data |

The system's job is to notice when Steven is doing layer 2, 3 or 4 work and move it down. That is the measured outcome, not the number of automations built.

## Standing rules
- The AI Team is draft-only: agents never send, sign, spend, book, trade or quote. Steven or Isa executes.
- Anything enters once, is classified once, is routed to the right owner or system, and resurfaces only when action or a decision is required.
- Only what Steven confirms is durable goes into the Second Brain; fetched or generated content goes in the answer.
- Client and deal folders (10-Clients, 20-Deals) are counted, never named, in any phone-sized answer.
- Whatever goes into Claude goes to Anthropic; sensitive client data may call for local models instead.
