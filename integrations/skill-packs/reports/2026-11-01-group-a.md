## Notes for Steven (plain English)

- **The cporter202 lists** are mostly advertising. Every link pays the author a referral fee, and most of the tools are scrapers. We keep one (the AI agent data catalog) as a bookmark and skip the rest, which matches how we handled his earlier lists.
- **Huginn** is a real, respected tool. It is a do-it-yourself automation server, though, and we already have Pabbly plus the Mac tasks. Bookmark it for now.
- **BillionMail** should not be installed. Its installer needs full admin control of a Linux server, weakens that server's security and skips download safety checks, and it does not run on a Mac at all. Running your own bulk mail server also brings anti-spam law and spam-folder problems. Your CRM's email drips are the safer path.
- **The Zillow MCP** needs an account and may cost money. It almost certainly scrapes Zillow, which breaks Zillow's terms. It also lets the AI sign up and pull up payment links by itself. Alexandra needs to review it, and it is your call. To read the vendor page in a cloud session, apillow.co has to be added to the allowed network domains first.
