## 2. Standing rules by regime

### Reg Z advertising (12 CFR 1026.24) — mortgage copy
- Trigger terms (a specific down payment amount or %, number of payments or term, a payment amount, a finance-charge amount) require: the down payment amount or %, the repayment terms, and the APR (and whether it may increase). The kit contains none; any future worked example (`docs/11` page 5) must either show the full set or state only "illustrative payment at an assumed rate" with the assumed rate, APR and date and Patriot Pacific's required disclosure block.
- A simple annual rate may only appear next to the APR, no more conspicuous than the APR.
- "VA loan" copy must not imply government endorsement: the non-affiliation line, no VA seal, no "official".
- Equal Housing Lender and the company NMLS ID on every mortgage ad; Steven's NMLS ID on anything that names him as the originator (12 CFR 1026.36(g): the loan originator's name and NMLSR ID on advertisements, business cards, websites).

### UDAAP / FTC Act
- No "best", "lowest", "guaranteed approval", "no credit check". The kit uses none of these; "best realtor" appears only inside a question people ask (H2), and the answer never claims it.
- Testimonials must be genuine; disclose any material connection; no incentives for reviews (16 CFR Part 465).

### CA DRE advertising (Business & Professions Code §10140.6; 10 CCR §2773)
- First point of contact materials (business cards, websites, social profiles, ads) must show: the licensee's name, license number, and the responsible broker's name (and the broker's license number where required). Standing line: `Steven Shearrill, Real Estate Broker, CA DRE #01988316 · LPT Realty, Inc., CA DRE #02248983`.
- The DRE number must be in a font size no smaller than the smallest type on the piece.
- "Veteran-owned" is a fact about Steven, not about LPT Realty; say "veteran-operated practice" or "owner is a Navy veteran" where LPT branding is required.

### NAR Code of Ethics, Article 12 and SOP 12-5
- Present a true picture; disclose the brokerage name on all advertising, including social posts and video descriptions.
- Mark REALTOR® with the ® on first use in formal copy.

### Fair Housing (FHA; CA FEHA)
- Describe property, amenities, commutes, costs, schools by name; never describe the people who live somewhere or who "fits" a neighborhood. "Military families" as an audience for *VA financing content* is fine; steering language about neighborhoods is not. Alexandra reviews pages 7 and 3.
- EHO logo/statement on real-estate pages.

### Dual capacity (agent + MLO on one file)
- Written disclosure before the buyer signs a representation agreement and before application; California requires the DRE "Disclosure of Dual Capacity" style notice where applicable; RESPA Section 8 affiliated-business rules if any referral fee or affiliation exists between LPT and Patriot Pacific (none is claimed in this kit). Wording: Patriot Pacific compliance + LPT's designated broker. The kit states "the required dual-capacity disclosures are provided up front" and that the client may use any lender or agent.

### TCPA / CAN-SPAM (review requests, follow-ups)
- Texts only to clients who gave consent; opt-out honored; emails with physical address and unsubscribe.

### Privacy
- No client name, photo, address or loan detail without written consent; no PII in schema, llms.txt or captions.
