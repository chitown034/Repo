## 8 · Mello-Roos in Temecula, Murrieta and Menifee

- Title: `Mello-Roos in Temecula, Murrieta and Menifee: What It Costs and How to Check | Steven Shearrill`
- H1: Mello-Roos explained for Temecula Valley buyers
- Answer: Mello-Roos is a special tax that funds infrastructure in Community Facilities Districts; many newer Temecula, Murrieta, Menifee and French Valley neighborhoods carry it, often several hundred dollars a month, and it does not appear in the list price.
- H2s: What is a CFD? · How do I find out if a home has Mello-Roos? · How long does it last? · Does the VA appraisal count it? · Does it affect my VA pre-approval? · Which neighborhoods have little or none?
- Specifics: Riverside County Treasurer-Tax Collector lookup steps; example tax bill line items.

## 10 · Using your VA loan twice

- Title: `Can You Use a VA Loan Twice? Entitlement, Second-Tier and Keeping Your First Home | Steven Shearrill`
- Answer: Yes. Entitlement can be restored after a sale, and remaining entitlement lets you hold two VA loans at once; the second purchase is limited by the county conforming limit when entitlement is partial.
- H2s: What is basic vs bonus entitlement? · How do I restore entitlement? · One-time restoration · Can I have two VA loans at once? · How is the max loan calculated with partial entitlement? · Does the funding fee change on a second use?
- Specifics: VA formula with a worked example using the current-year Riverside and San Diego limits (dated, FHFA link).
