## 1. Why citations still matter in 2026

Google's local pack, Gemini's "near me" answers, Apple Maps/Siri, Bing/Copilot and every "best realtor in Temecula" answer from ChatGPT or Perplexity lean on the same thing: many independent sources agreeing on one name, one phone, one city, one category. Each directory below is one more vote. Conflicting votes (eXp vs LPT, Winchester vs Temecula) cost more than missing votes.

## 7. Review engine (Victor owns the ask; Alexandra owns the wording)

- Review links: Google (short link from GBP), Zillow (review request tool), Yelp (never solicit; Yelp penalizes asked-for reviews), Facebook, Homes.com.
- Timing: at the closing table (verbal), same-day text (Google link), day 7 email (Zillow link), day 30 anniversary note (Facebook).
- Wording rule: ask for an honest review; never offer anything of value for a review (FTC 16 CFR Part 465, effective October 2024, prohibits buying reviews and suppressing negative ones); never ask only happy clients while gating unhappy ones ("review gating").
- Mortgage reviews: Patriot Pacific may have its own review platform (Experience.com, SocialSurvey, Birdeye). Ask; those feed Zillow lender and Google.
- Response template (Google): "Thank you, [first name]. Helping a [Navy/Marine/Army] family buy in [Temecula/Murrieta] with a VA loan is exactly why I do this. Enjoy the new home. — Steven Shearrill, LPT Realty · Patriot Pacific Financial"

## 9. What to never do

- No PBNs, no paid "directory bundles", no bulk citation submissions with inconsistent data.
- No fake or incentivized reviews, no review gating, no "review stations" in the office.
- No listing a home address as an office if clients cannot visit by appointment.
- No category stuffing (Google suspends profiles for unrelated categories).
