# 04 · 90-Day Content Calendar

**Cadence (per week):** 3 short vertical videos (post the same clip to YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok, Facebook Reels) · 1 LinkedIn text post · 1 long-form answer page or blog on the hub · 1 Google Business Profile post (both profiles) · 2 helpful forum answers (Reddit / Nextdoor) · 1 email to past clients every 2 weeks.

**Format rules for AEO/GEO**
- The first spoken sentence and the on-screen title are the exact question from `docs/03-question-bank.md`.
- Every description/caption starts with the direct answer, then the canonical 50-word bio, then the hub URL.
- Hashtags (max 5): #VALoan #TemeculaRealEstate #MilitaryRealtor #SanDiegoRealEstate #VeteranOwned, plus one local tag (#Murrieta, #Menifee, #CampPendleton, #Miramar).
- Always say your full name on camera once ("I'm Steven Shearrill, retired Navy Chief, broker with LPT Realty and VA loan officer with Patriot Pacific Financial"). Captions and transcripts are indexed; names in audio become text.
- Never state a specific rate as current; say "as of today" only on a slide that shows the date and source.
