Military Families' Guide to Buying in Temecula Valley

By Steven Shearrill, retired Navy ChiefReal Estate Broker, DRE #01988316VA Loan Officer, NMLS #2449246Updated 22 Oct 2026

Temecula Valley gives Camp Pendleton, Miramar and Naval Base San Diego families larger, newer homes for less than coastal San Diego County, in exchange for a 35 to 75 minute commute and, in newer tracts, Mello-Roos taxes. Here is how to weigh it, neighborhood by neighborhood, with the VA loan built in.

I bought and sold here through my own PCS moves, and I now represent buyers as a Broker with LPT Realty and write their VA loans as a loan officer with Patriot Pacific Financial. Prices and commute times change; every figure below says when it was checked.

Mello-Roos: the line item that surprises buyers

Mello-Roos (a Community Facilities District special tax) funds schools, roads and parks in newer tracts. It sits on the property tax bill, not the list price, and the VA appraisal does not flag it. In parts of Temecula, Murrieta, Menifee and French Valley it adds several hundred dollars a month. Before any offer, pull the parcel's tax bill from the Riverside County Treasurer-Tax Collector and put the real monthly figure into your VA pre-approval. Older neighborhoods and most of Fallbrook have little or none.

BAH, housing areas and the VA loan

  • BAH follows your duty station. A Camp Pendleton Marine living in Temecula is paid the Camp Pendleton housing-area rate, not Riverside County's.
  • BAH counts as income. Because it is tax-free, lenders may gross it up (VA Lender's Handbook, Chapter 4; the percentage is lender policy).
  • No down payment is required by the VA for eligible borrowers and there is no monthly mortgage insurance; lender credit and property requirements apply.
  • Keep it when you PCS. After meeting the occupancy rule, you may rent the home out on orders and use remaining entitlement at the next station. See how VA loans work here.
Rule of thumb

Set the full monthly payment, including Mello-Roos and HOA, at or under your BAH with-dependents rate. Then the home pays for itself while you are here and rents near break-even when you leave. Run the numbers before you tour.

Schools and base access

  • Temecula Valley USD, Murrieta Valley USD, Menifee Union / Perris Union High, and Fallbrook Union districts serve the valley; boundaries change, so confirm the assigned school for a specific address with the district before relying on a listing's claim.
  • Camp Pendleton's Naval Hospital, commissary and exchange are a 35–45 minute drive from Temecula; VA Loma Linda and VA San Diego both have clinics within reach of the valley.
  • March Air Reserve Base (Riverside) is about 30 minutes north of Menifee for Reserve and Guard families.

PCS timeline: orders to keys

  1. Day 0–2: Certificate of Eligibility pulled; LES, orders and two years of history reviewed; VA pre-approval issued.
  2. Day 2–14: Tour in person or by video walkthrough; offers written with the real payment including Mello-Roos and HOA.
  3. Day 14–17: Offer accepted; VA appraisal ordered the same day.
  4. Day 17–35: Appraisal, inspections, underwriting; power of attorney arranged if you are deployed or in transit.
  5. Day 35–45: Clear to close, signing (mobile notary or remote where allowed), funding, keys.

Questions

How far is Temecula from Camp Pendleton?

About 35 to 45 minutes by car via CA-79 and I-15 to the Oceanside main gate at 0600, longer at peak. Fallbrook and the De Luz area are closer to the back gates, which open on base policy.

Is Temecula a good place for military families?

Many Camp Pendleton and Miramar families choose Temecula Valley for larger newer homes at lower prices than coastal San Diego County, with the trade-off of a longer commute and, in newer tracts, Mello-Roos taxes. BAH follows the duty station, not the home address.

What is Mello-Roos and do Temecula homes have it?

Mello-Roos is a special tax that funds infrastructure in Community Facilities Districts. Many newer neighborhoods in Temecula, Murrieta, Menifee and French Valley carry it, often several hundred dollars a month; older neighborhoods usually do not. Check the property tax bill before you offer.

Which BAH rate applies if I live in Temecula but work at Camp Pendleton?

BAH is based on your duty station, not your home address. A service member assigned to Camp Pendleton receives the Camp Pendleton housing area rate even when living in Temecula or Murrieta.

Does California tax military retirement pay?

Yes. California taxes military retirement pay as ordinary income. Disabled veterans may qualify for a California property tax exemption on a primary residence; check the current amounts with the county assessor.

Can I buy a home in Temecula before I arrive on orders?

Yes. With orders, a Certificate of Eligibility and a VA pre-approval, you can tour by video, write an offer and close in about 30 to 45 days, signing remotely or through a specific power of attorney if you are deployed or in transit.
