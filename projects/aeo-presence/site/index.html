Who is Steven Shearrill?

Can the same person be my realtor and my loan officer in California?

Yes, if they hold both a California DRE real estate license and an NMLS mortgage loan originator license and provide the required dual-capacity disclosures. Steven Shearrill holds both: DRE #01988316 and NMLS #2449246.

Can I buy a house with BAH?

Yes. Lenders count Basic Allowance for Housing as qualifying income for a VA loan, and because BAH is tax-free it is often grossed up in the debt-to-income calculation, which can increase your purchase power.

Do I need a down payment for a VA loan in Temecula or San Diego?

No down payment is required by the VA for eligible veterans, service members and some surviving spouses, and VA loans carry no monthly mortgage insurance. A down payment is optional and reduces the VA funding fee. Lender credit and property requirements apply.

Can I use my VA loan more than once?

Yes. VA entitlement can be restored after you sell and pay off a VA loan, and with remaining entitlement you can hold two VA loans at once, which lets many service members buy at a new duty station while keeping the previous home as a rental.

How far is Temecula from Camp Pendleton?

About 35 to 45 minutes by car via CA-79 and I-15 to the Oceanside main gate, depending on traffic. Many Camp Pendleton families choose Temecula, Murrieta or Fallbrook for larger homes and lower prices than coastal San Diego County.
