VA Home Loans in Temecula and San Diego

By Steven Shearrill, retired Navy ChiefVA Loan Officer, NMLS #2449246Real Estate Broker, DRE #01988316Updated 12 Oct 2026

A VA home loan lets eligible service members, veterans and some surviving spouses buy a primary residence in Temecula, Murrieta, Menifee or anywhere in San Diego County with no down payment and no monthly mortgage insurance. Your tax-free BAH counts as income, there is no VA loan limit if you have full entitlement, and you can use the benefit more than once.

I write these loans as a Mortgage Loan Originator with Patriot Pacific Financial in Temecula and represent buyers as a Broker with LPT Realty. This page covers what actually matters locally. Nothing here is a rate quote; program rules change, so verify current figures at va.gov or ask me.

Who is eligible

  • Active duty after 90 continuous days of service.
  • Veterans who meet the minimum service requirement for their era and have an other-than-dishonorable discharge.
  • National Guard and Reserve members, generally after six years of service or 90 days of active service under Title 10 orders (rules differ by period of service).
  • Un-remarried surviving spouses of veterans who died in service or from a service-connected disability, and some spouses of veterans who are MIA or POW.

Proof is your Certificate of Eligibility (COE). As your loan officer I can usually pull it electronically in minutes with your DD-214 or a Statement of Service.

Zero down, and what it costs instead

VA loans finance 100% of the purchase price. Instead of mortgage insurance, the VA charges a one-time funding fee that is normally rolled into the loan. The percentage depends on whether it is your first VA loan and whether you put money down; it is waived for veterans receiving VA disability compensation, active-duty Purple Heart recipients and eligible surviving spouses. Current percentages are published by the VA each year.

Local specific

Many newer subdivisions in Temecula, Murrieta, Menifee and French Valley carry Mello-Roos (CFD) taxes that can add several hundred dollars a month to your payment. The VA appraisal does not flag them; your pre-approval should. I run every home you tour with the actual tax bill, not the county base rate.

BAH as income

Basic Allowance for Housing is counted as qualifying income. Because it is tax-free, most lenders gross it up (commonly by 15–25%) in the debt-to-income calculation, which raises the price you qualify for compared with the same gross pay in a civilian job. The same applies to BAS and other non-taxable allowances.

Rule of thumb for planning: BAH with dependents for the San Diego and Riverside County military housing areas is published each December by the Department of Defense. Set the mortgage payment, including Mello-Roos and HOA, at or under the BAH figure and the home pays for itself while you are stationed here.

This is the move many service members miss: buy in Temecula with VA, PCS, keep the home as a rental, then use remaining entitlement to buy at the next station. Occupancy rules require you to move in within 60 days and intend to live there; once you receive orders, renting it out is allowed.

2–4 units with a VA loan

The VA allows a duplex, triplex or fourplex if you live in one unit. Projected rent from the other units can be counted toward qualifying (lenders usually require a reserve cushion and may ask for landlord experience or a property-management plan). Inland Empire multi-unit inventory is limited but it exists in older parts of Temecula, Murrieta and Lake Elsinore, and more widely in Oceanside, Escondido and San Diego.

New construction, condos and the appraisal

  • New builds in Menifee, French Valley and Winchester: most national builders accept VA financing and some offer incentives; a one-time-close VA construction loan is possible on custom builds.
  • Condos must be in a VA-approved project. Many San Diego complexes are approved; if yours is not, the lender can submit it for approval, which adds time.
  • VA appraisal checks value and Minimum Property Requirements (safety, soundness, sanitation). If the appraiser expects to come in below contract price, the Tidewater process gives the lender 48 hours to submit comparables before the value is final.

PCS timeline: orders to keys in 45 days

  1. Day 0–2: COE pulled, LES and two years of history reviewed, pre-approval issued.
  2. Day 2–14: Tour in person or by video walkthrough; offers written with real payment numbers including Mello-Roos and HOA.
  3. Day 14–17: Offer accepted; disclosures issued; appraisal ordered immediately.
  4. Day 17–35: Appraisal, inspections, underwriting. Power of attorney arranged if you are deployed or in transit.
  5. Day 35–45: Clear to close, sign (mobile notary or remote where allowed), fund, keys.

Questions

Can I use a VA loan to buy a home in Temecula?

Yes. A VA loan can be used for a primary residence anywhere in Temecula, Murrieta or Menifee, with no down payment and no monthly mortgage insurance for eligible veterans, service members and some surviving spouses.

Is there a VA loan limit in San Diego County or Riverside County?

Not for borrowers with full entitlement; since 2020 the VA sets no loan limit for them and lenders set their own caps. Borrowers with partial entitlement (an existing VA loan) are limited by the county conforming loan limit published each January by the FHFA.

Can I use a VA loan on a duplex or fourplex in Temecula?

Yes. The VA allows 1 to 4 unit properties if you occupy one unit as your primary residence, and projected rent from the other units can be used to help you qualify.

What is the VA funding fee?

A one-time fee paid to the VA, usually financed into the loan, that depends on down payment and whether it is your first VA loan. It is waived for veterans receiving VA disability compensation, Purple Heart recipients on active duty and eligible surviving spouses. Current percentages are published at va.gov.

Can I rent out my VA-financed home when I PCS?

Yes. After satisfying the occupancy requirement, generally moving in within 60 days with the intent to live there, you may keep the VA loan and rent the home when you receive orders, and use remaining entitlement to buy at the next duty station.

How long does a VA loan take to close in California?

Typically 30 to 45 days from accepted offer. The VA appraisal is the step most likely to add time; if the appraiser expects a low value, the Tidewater process gives the lender 48 hours to submit comparable sales.
