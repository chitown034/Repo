# Military relocation

PCS timing, orders, tour length, the rent/buy/wait gate. **Status: stub, 2026-09-28.**

## What is verified — and actually built — in this repository

- The **Rent, Buy, or Wait** military housing dashboard is a real, separate artifact maintained by
  the `rent-buy-dashboard-refresh` skill (`.claude/skills/`): "refresh every time-sensitive figure...
  and republish it," including the January BAH rollover. This is the working tool for the rent/buy/
  wait gate named in this page's own summary — route there for the live decision, not here.
- `wiki/mortgage-programs/va-affordability.md` covers the BAH-affordability side of the same
  question; this page is the relocation-timing side.
- `projects/nonprofit.md` lists a researched (not yet active) partnership: **PenFed Foundation —
  Military Heroes Program.** Tangential to relocation itself, but the closest thing in this
  repository to a named military-support partner, worth surfacing on a relocation question.

PCS orders/tour-length mechanics beyond the dashboard above are not recorded in this repository.
