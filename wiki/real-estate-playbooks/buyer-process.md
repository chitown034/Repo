## The one standing fact worth repeating here

**Earnest money is a deadline, not a detail.** A missed EMD deadline can put a buyer in default.
This topic's own index already states it as a real gap the improvement loop found and patched into
both the deck and the ISA Portal — any stage description of this process that omits EMD is wrong.
See `wiki/real-estate-playbooks/index.md` and `wiki/real-estate-playbooks/transaction-stages.md`.
