## The one working BAH tool already in this stack

The `rent-buy-dashboard-refresh` skill (`.claude/skills/`) maintains a separate artifact — the
**Rent, Buy, or Wait** military housing dashboard — that already does BAH-based math (by
locality, refreshed on the BAH January rollover). It is not this wiki page and not a substitute for
a guideline citation, but it is the closest real, working BAH logic in this stack and the right
place to point a "what would this PCS cost to rent vs. buy" question. See also
`wiki/real-estate-playbooks/military-relocation.md`.
