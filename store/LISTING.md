**Description**

```
Serving those who served.

Active Duty Veteran and Hometown Hero Home-Owners Club, Inc. is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit that gives active duty service members, veterans, military families, and hometown heroes (firefighters, police officers, EMTs, nurses, and teachers) free education and tools. Everything in the app is free, with no account needed.

BENEFITS FINDER
Search 2,345 federal, state, and private benefits by state, category, and level. Save the ones that matter to you.

VA HOME BUYING
• VA loan payment, funding fee, and BAH buying power calculators
• Combined VA disability rating calculator
• Register for the free VA home buying webinar: 10 common mistakes military VA homebuyers make, and how to avoid them

CAREERS
• Career Command Center: search job boards, track applications, and practice interview questions
• Guides for resumes, interviews, certifications, apprenticeships, and more

RESOURCES
Every guide from veteransresourcenetwork.org: veteran resources, mental health, housing and utility help, education and scholarships, relocation, retirement planning, and county guides for San Diego, Riverside, Orange, and Los Angeles.

PAY IT FORWARD
Know a veteran who should be using their VA home loan benefit? Pick friends from your contacts and each one gets their own text introducing them to Steven Shearrill, a retired U.S. Navy Chief. Your contacts stay on your phone.

HELP WHEN YOU NEED IT
Veterans Crisis Line: call 988 and press 1, or text 838255.

We are an independent nonprofit and are not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs.
```

**Notes for App Review**

```
No sign-in is required; every feature is available right away.

Pay It Forward (Home > Pay It Forward): the app asks for Contacts access, lists the phone's contacts, and the user ticks friends. For each friend, the app opens the standard Messages compose sheet with that friend and our founder (619-368-7141) as recipients and a pre-written introduction. Nothing is sent unless the user taps Send. Contacts are never uploaded.

Donate opens our PayPal donation page in Safari, outside the app. We are a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 84-3348125.

Forms (contact, webinar registration) open the user's email app with their message; there is no server account.
```
